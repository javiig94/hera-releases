How to use this

Rules before you copy anything

These are skeletons, not scripts. Change the words to yours. A woman can tell a template from a man in two lines. Every message here follows the same five laws:

  1. Short. Under four lines. Always.
  2. Light. No feelings, no relationship, no "we need to talk".
  3. Specific. One real detail she'd recognise beats ten compliments.
  4. No pressure. Easy to answer, easy to ignore. Both are fine.
  5. End first. You close the conversation. Every time.

Timing

Mid-week, 6–8 p.m. Never after 10 p.m. Never after a drink. Reply hours later, not seconds. If she takes a day, you take a day.

Never sendAnything with "please", "just", "I can't", "I miss you", "do you still…", or a question about her feelings. Anything over four lines. Anything you wouldn't send knowing she'll show her best friend.
Part 1 · Day 0–2

The closing message

Send one. Then silence for 30 days. Purpose: replace the last memory of a desperate man with a composed one.

01 · StandardHey. I've had some time to think and I want to say this once, properly: you were right to end it, and I'm sorry for how I handled it afterwards. I'm going to give us both space. I genuinely hope you're doing well.
02 · If you begged / argued a lotI owe you a clean one: the way I reacted after we ended wasn't fair on you. That's on me. I'm stepping back properly now. Take care of yourself.
03 · If she ended it coldlyGot it. I won't keep pushing — you've made your decision and I respect it. Wishing you well.
04 · If there's logistics (keys, stuff, lease)Quick practical one: I'll leave your things with [friend / at the door] on [day]. No need to reply unless that doesn't work. Hope you're ok.
05 · If you share a pet / childKeeping this strictly about [name]: [practical info]. I'll keep everything else out of these messages so it's easy for both of us.

If she replies to any of these: "Thank you, that means a lot. Take care." — and nothing more.

Part 5

The "us" conversation

Let her raise it if you can. Say the honest script once, clearly, and then stay exactly who you walked in as.

35 · The honest script (in person)I'm not going to pretend the last few months didn't happen. I took a hard look at who I'd become and I didn't like him much. So I've been changing it — for me, not to get you back. But I'd be lying if I said I didn't still care about you. If you ever wanted to see what this looks like now, I'd be up for that. If not, I understand, and I'm genuinely glad we can still sit here like this.
36 · She brings up the breakup on the first meetingYeah. You were right about a lot of that. I've been working on it. But I didn't come to relitigate anything — tell me about [her thing].
37 · "What's different now?"Honestly? I stopped making you my whole life and started having one. [One concrete example.] You can judge the rest for yourself.
38 · "I need time"Of course. Take whatever time you need — I'm not going anywhere in a hurry, and I'm not waiting around either. Door's open.
39 · "I don't want to get hurt again"That's fair. I'm not asking you to trust me on words. Watch what I do for a while and decide then.
40 · "Can we take it slow?"I'd prefer that, honestly. Let's do it properly this time.
41 · She says yesGood. Then let's do it properly. I'll plan Saturday.
42 · She says noI understand, and I respect it. Thank you for being straight with me. I genuinely wish you well.
43 · She says no, then texts a week laterHey — good to hear from you. Just so we're straight, I'm not going to keep going back and forth; that's not good for either of us. If something's changed, say so. If not, no hard feelings at all.

Part 7 · Emergency

When the urge hits, you don't text her. You text one of these to the notebook, or to your accountability man.

THE 2 A.M.
RELAPSE PROTOCOL

Read top to bottom. Do each step before the next. Do not skip to the phone.

  1. 0:00 — Phone face-down, other room. Physically move it. You can't win a fight you keep in your hand.
  2. 0:01 — Say it out loud: "This is chemistry, not information." Withdrawal feels like truth. It isn't.
  3. 0:02 — Cold water on face and wrists, 60 seconds. Then 20 slow breaths, four counts in, six out.
  4. 0:05 — Notebook, four lines: Feeling / Thought / What I'd text / What a man I respect does instead.
  5. 0:10 — Text your accountability man (Script 54), not her. If he's asleep, the message still counts.
  6. 0:12 — Move: 50 push-ups or a 10-minute walk outside. Thought follows action.
  7. 0:25 — Decide one thing for tomorrow and write it down: the session, the mission block, the call you'll make.
  8. 0:30 — Sleep. No decision made after 9 p.m. is a decision. Re-read this at 7 a.m. if you still want to send it. You won't.
If you already sent itScript 57 in the morning. Then day 1 again. One message doesn't undo thirty days of work — but a second one starts to.
